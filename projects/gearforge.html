GearForge - Two-Stage Spur Gearbox

A complete gearbox project covering CAD, detailed component design, machining, assembly, tolerance checks, BOM development, and functional testing.

DESIGN

From component models to a complete transmission assembly

I designed the gearbox components in SolidWorks and AutoCAD and developed the complete two-stage spur-gear arrangement, including the shafts, gears, housing, and assembly interfaces.

MANUFACTURING

Machining and fit control

The components were manufactured using milling, drilling, and turning. I checked tolerance fits, shaft and gear alignment, and surface finish during assembly so the manufactured system matched the design intent.

VALIDATION

Testing against the design calculations

I prepared the BOM, selected materials, estimated project cost, documented the manufacturing process, and tested the finished gearbox to compare measured speed ratio and torque transmission with the theoretical design calculations.
