2025 Formula Student Vehicle

AERODYNAMIC DEVELOPMENT

Front-wing design as a junior member

I designed the front wing and supported the team member working on the rear wing. Through the front-wing development process, I reduced the front-wing drag by approximately 150 N in the team's simulations while maintaining the aerodynamic function required for the vehicle.

I also ran the structural stress simulations required by the Formula Student rules for the relevant aerodynamic components so the concepts were checked against the competition load cases.

MANUFACTURING & ASSEMBLY

From CAD to physical integration

I planned and oversaw the aerodynamic manufacturing process, mounting strategy, and assembly. This included coordinating how the components would be produced, how they would attach to the vehicle, and how the aero package would be assembled without creating conflicts with the rolling chassis or electronics.

LOW VOLTAGE COLLABORATION

Mechanical packaging and waterproofing

I worked with the Low Voltage team to create and waterproof the battery enclosure, PCB enclosure, and dashboard. This gave me early experience working across mechanical and electrical requirements rather than treating the body system as an isolated subsystem.
