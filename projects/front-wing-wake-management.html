Front Wing - Wake Management Development

A front-wing redesign focused on controlling wake behavior to reduce the additional drag introduced by the new vehicle assembly.

~200 NVehicle drag reduction in simulation
#1Highest aero efficiency among iterations
4Simulation platforms used across aero development
RuleStress load cases checked

PROBLEM

Drag was being driven by wake behavior

The new vehicle assembly introduced additional drag because of how the front-wing flow interacted with the rest of the car. Rather than optimizing the front wing only for its own local forces, I treated it as a wake-management device and focused on improving the flow seen by the downstream vehicle.

SOLUTION

Using the front wing to manage downstream flow

I iterated the wing geometry around wake control and used the vehicle simulations to evaluate the effect on the complete car. The final wake-management approach reduced vehicle drag by approximately 200 N in simulation and achieved the highest aerodynamic efficiency among the front-wing iterations developed by the team.

VALIDATION

Simulation and structural verification

I handled the theoretical side of the analysis, including model selection, verification, validation, and result interpretation. I also ran the structural stress simulations mandated by the Formula Student rules for the relevant wing components to verify the design against the required load cases.
