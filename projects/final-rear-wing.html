FORMULA STUDENT · AERODYNAMICS

Final Rear-Wing Iteration

A mass-optimized rear wing that retained almost all of the downforce of the 4-element concept while reducing drag, weight, and assembly complexity.

OPTIMIZATION TARGET

Keep the downforce, remove the penalties

The previous 4-element rear wing achieved about 350 N of downforce, but its drag and 3 kg mass increase made it unsuitable as the final configuration. I redesigned the wing around a better complete-vehicle compromise rather than maximizing a single aerodynamic output.

RESULT

About 320 to 330 N of downforce with 4 kg less mass

The final iteration gave up only about 20 to 30 N of downforce compared with the 350 N high-downforce concept. In return, it marginally reduced drag and cut approximately 4 kg of mass relative to that previous wing.

DESIGN FOR ASSEMBLY

Endplates designed to simplify integration

I also refined the endplate design to make the rear wing easier to assemble and integrate. This meant the final design addressed aerodynamic performance, structural requirements, weight, and practical assembly at the same time.

I ran the structural stress simulations mandated by the Formula Student rules for the relevant rear-wing components before the design was treated as structurally acceptable.
