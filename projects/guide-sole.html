Guide Sole - Wearable Navigation Device

A wearable obstacle-awareness prototype combining mechanical design, ultrasonic sensing, vibration feedback, audible warnings, and Arduino control.

WEARABLE PROTOTYPE

Guide Sole

Mechanical design + embedded sensing + user feedback

OBJECTIVE

Obstacle awareness through a wearable system

The project explored a wearable navigation concept that could detect nearby obstacles and communicate warnings through vibration and sound rather than requiring the user to continuously watch a display.

SYSTEM INTEGRATION

Mechanical and electronic design in one prototype

I worked with ultrasonic sensors, vibration motors, buzzers, and an Arduino Nano and integrated the electronics into a mechanically designed wearable concept. The project required both CAD development and embedded programming rather than treating the housing and electronics as separate tasks.
